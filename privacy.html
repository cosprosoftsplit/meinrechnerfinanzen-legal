Privacy Policy

MeinRechnerFinanzen — Last updated 2026-04-10

Introduction

MeinRechnerFinanzen is developed by Cosmic Production d.o.o., based in Split, Croatia. We respect your privacy and aim to keep this app as simple and data-light as possible.

Local Calculations and History

Your calculation inputs, results, and local history are stored on your device. We do not require an account and we do not run our own backend for user data.

Advertising

The free version of the app may display banner ads through Google AdMob.

  • If you allow tracking on iOS, advertising partners may use device identifiers to provide more relevant ads.
  • If you do not allow tracking, the app may still show less personalised ads.
  • For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Premium is offered as a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Apple handles payments, restoration, and any refund workflow under Apple's policies.

Analytics

We do not run our own analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app code. Apple may provide aggregated App Store analytics to us through App Store Connect.

Your Choices

You can delete the app at any time. You can also clear your local calculation history from inside the app. Tracking permission can be managed in iOS Settings.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at ivan@cosmicproduction.hr.
